Where the data comes from

  • Chlorophyll‑a — VIIRS on NOAA‑21, via NASA GIBS. Daily, roughly 1 km.
  • Sea surface temperature — GHRSST Level 4 MUR, via NASA GIBS. Daily blended analysis.
  • Bathymetry — Esri Ocean Basemap and GEBCO.
  • Wind, swell, current — Open‑Meteo forecast and marine models.
  • Tide — harmonic constituents from the Neaps tide database (TICON‑4), computed on the phone.

Cloud blanks out the satellite layers. If today looks empty, wind the date back a day or two. Imagery is an aid, not a navigation instrument.
